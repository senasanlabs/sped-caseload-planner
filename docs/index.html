How to use it

  1. Download it first. Save the file to your computer or tablet. Previews inside email or Google Drive can’t fill in forms.
  2. Open it in a PDF reader. Adobe Acrobat Reader (free), Apple Preview, or the PDF viewer built into Chrome or Edge.
  3. Type each name once. Enter a student’s name on the Caseload Roster. It fills in automatically on that student’s goal tracker and meeting notes pages.
  4. Save after each session. Print any page when you need a paper copy. Every page is sized for US Letter.

Your student information stays with you. This page has no sign-up, no tracking and no analytics. Everything you type lives only in your own copy of the PDF, so store and share it according to your district’s policies.

About the planner

The Special Education Teacher IEP & 504 Caseload Planner is a 280-page, 8.5 × 11 in paperback for a 30-student caseload. It has twelve tabbed sections, dated calendars from July 2026 to July 2027, small-group planning pages, and goal, meeting and data trackers. It’s an organizational planner, not legal or compliance advice.
